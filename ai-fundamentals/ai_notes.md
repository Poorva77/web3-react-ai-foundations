# AI Fundamentals

## What is Artificial Intelligence?

Artificial Intelligence (AI) enables machines to simulate human intelligence.

---

## Types of AI

### Narrow AI
Designed for specific tasks.

### General AI
Can perform multiple human-level tasks.

### Super AI
Advanced intelligence beyond humans.

---

## Machine Learning

Machine Learning is a subset of AI where systems learn from data.

---

## Real-world Applications

- Chatbots
- Recommendation Systems
- Self-driving Cars
- Face Recognition
- Healthcare Prediction

---

## Benefits of AI

- Automation
- Faster decision making
- Reduced human effort
- Improved accuracy
